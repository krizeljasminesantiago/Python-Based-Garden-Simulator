📡 Treasure detector
How it works:
1️⃣ A 💎 is buried in ONE plot of your garden today. The yellow box shows where it could be.
2️⃣ The box is split into two halves: A (blue) and B (pink). Press a scan button: it tells you if the 💎 is in that half. Either way the search area shrinks to the half that has it.
3️⃣ Keep scanning (each scan costs 4 Wh of sunlight) until ONE plot is left. Every scan cuts the area in half. This is divide and conquer, like guessing a number.
4️⃣ Click that plot in the garden, then press the shovel (X) to dig it up.
